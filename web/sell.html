Common questions

Is listing really free?

Yes. The form and a basic listing cost nothing. We make money when a buyer connects to a property, not by charging you to post.

Will buyers get my phone number?

No. Buyers see only our number and message us. We pass on your details after you agree to talk, so you never get unwanted calls.

Do you take a cut when it sells?

Only if you ask us to handle the deal, and we agree the percentage with you first. Listing alone is free and carries no obligation.

How long does it take to go live?

Usually same day once we have confirmed the details and photos.

What if my property is already on a portal?

That is fine. We do not ask you to remove it anywhere. Our listing is extra reach, especially for buyers searching by area and budget.
